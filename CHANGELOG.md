# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-09

- QPC-1 derivation standard, byte-compatible with `$QUANTUM`
- `qpc` CLI: `derive`, `commit`, `analyze`, `launch`, `certificate`, `verify`, `export-keypair`
- Token-2022 launch with on-chain quantum metadata; mint & freeze authority revoked by default
- IBM Quantum bit harvester (`quantum/generate_bits.py`) and independent Python verifier
- `$QUANTUM` genesis data, birth certificate, bit mosaic and qubit heatmap
- Registry of verified coins with CI re-verification
